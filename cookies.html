Cookies and browser storage

StudyFlow sets no cookies. It does keep a few things in your browser so the app works, and this page lists every one of them.

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
  • Everything below is stored only in your own browser, read only by this site, and never sent to another company.
  • All of it is either needed for the app to work or something you chose, such as dark mode. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing optional to agree to.

What is stored in your browser

NameWhat it is forHow long
studyflow-data-v1 Your subjects, deadlines, CAS entries, EE notes and study hours, so the app works on this device and offline. Until you use Start over, log out of an account, or clear your browser data.
studyflow-auth-v1 Your login session, only if you have an account. Without it you would have to log in on every visit. Until you log out.
studyflow-sync-v1 A short fingerprint of what was last saved to your account, so the app knows whether there are changes to upload. Until you log out.
studyflow-signed-out Remembers that you logged out of a device-only diploma, so the start screen shows instead of your data. Until you open the diploma again.
studyflow-local-only Remembers that you chose to use StudyFlow without an account, so you are not asked again. Until you clear your browser data.
studyflow-theme Light or dark mode, if you picked one. Until you clear your browser data.
Offline copy of the site The site's own pages, styles and scripts, saved by a service worker so StudyFlow opens without a connection. It holds no personal data. Replaced each time the site updates.

How to clear it

  • Without an account: Settings → Start over deletes your diploma from this browser.
  • With an account: logging out removes your diploma and your session from this browser. Settings → Delete my account removes it from our side too.
  • Everything at once: clear this site's data in your browser settings.

If this changes

If StudyFlow ever adds anything optional, such as analytics, it will ask you first, with a choice that is as easy to refuse as to accept, and it will be listed here before it goes live.

More detail on what is held with an account, and who to contact, is on the privacy page.
