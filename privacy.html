Privacy

What StudyFlow stores

Written to match what the software actually does. If you find a difference between this page and the app's behaviour, the app is wrong and we want to know.

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

  • Without an account, everything stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • With an account, we store your email, your name if you give one, and the diploma data you enter.
  • There are no adverts, no trackers, no analytics and no third-party scripts of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for marketing. There is nobody to sell it to.
  • You can delete the whole account, and everything in it, from Settings.

Using StudyFlow without an account

If you do not sign in, your subjects, deadlines, CAS entries, Extended Essay notes and study hours are saved in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data erases them, and we cannot get them back.

What we do not hold

No date of birth, no school, no location, no phone number, no payment details, no contacts, no browsing history, no device fingerprint, no advertising identifier. We do not keep a log of pages you visit inside the app. We do not record your IP address against your account; it is used only in memory, briefly, to limit repeated login attempts.

Your password

Your password is never stored. What is stored is a scrypt hash of it with a random salt — a one-way scramble that cannot be reversed into the original. Nobody operating this service can read your password, recover it, or tell you what it was. That is why a forgotten password needs a reset rather than a reminder.

Who else can see it

Two companies hold data on our behalf because the site runs on their infrastructure:

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores the account database.
  • Resend delivers password-reset emails, and therefore sees the email address a reset is sent to. It is used for nothing else.

If this deployment has been configured to use Supabase instead, Supabase holds the accounts and the data in place of Netlify's storage.

Nobody else. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no data broker. The pages load no third-party scripts at all, so no other company is in a position to observe you using StudyFlow.

Honest limits

Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected in transit and at rest by the hosting platform, and the database refuses to return one account's rows to another — but whoever administers this deployment can read the database. If that is not acceptable to you, use StudyFlow without an account and your data never leaves your device.

How long it is kept

Your account data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account from Settings removes the login and the diploma data immediately and permanently, including any reset link already sent. There is no archive and no soft-delete, so it cannot be undone.

Password-reset links expire after one hour or on first use, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — and under the UK and EU GDPR in particular — you have the right to see what is held about you, to correct it, to have it erased, to object to processing, and to take your data elsewhere. StudyFlow is built so you can exercise most of these yourself:

  • See it: everything we hold is visible in the app. There is no hidden profile.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app.
  • Erase it: Settings → Delete my account.
  • Take it elsewhere: your data is a plain JSON document; ask and we will send you a copy of it.

For anything that is not covered by a button in the app, contact whoever runs this deployment using the address below.

Students under 18

StudyFlow is built for IB Diploma Programme students, many of whom are under 18. We ask for as little as the software can function with — an email address and a password — and nothing that identifies a person beyond that.

We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. In parts of the EU and the UK, children under 16 need a parent or guardian's consent before using a service like this; if you are under 16, please ask them first. If you believe a child has an account that should not exist, contact us and it will be deleted.

If a school deploys StudyFlow for its students, the school is responsible for the lawful basis on which it does so, and should say so in its own privacy notice.

Cookies

StudyFlow sets no cookies and uses no cookie banners, because it has nothing to consent to. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your diploma on your device and, if you are signed in, your login session. That storage is readable only by this site, is never sent to any third party, and is cleared when you log out or clear your browser data.

Changes

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. Material changes to how your data is used will be announced in the app rather than quietly edited in here.
