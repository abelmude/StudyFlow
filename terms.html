What StudyFlow is

A tool for keeping track of your own IB Diploma Programme deadlines. It is a planner. It does not submit work, does not talk to your school, and has no authority over anything that counts.

It is not the IB, and not your school

StudyFlow is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the International Baccalaureate Organization, and "International Baccalaureate", "IB" and "IB Diploma Programme" are their trademarks, used here only to describe what the tool is for.

Nothing here is official guidance. Deadlines, word counts, CAS requirements and assessment rules come from your school and the IB, and where this app disagrees with them, they are right and it is wrong. Always check the real thing.

  • You are responsible for what happens under your login.
  • One person per account. Do not share a login with your class.
  • If you think someone else has got in, change your password — that signs every other device out.

What not to do

  • Do not try to reach anybody else's account or data.
  • Do not attack, overload or probe the service, or work around its limits.

  • Do not use it to harass anybody. The message drafts are for your own deadlines.

Messages to teachers

StudyFlow can draft a message about a deadline that is slipping. It never sends one. Sending opens your own mail program, and what you send is yours — read it before you do.
