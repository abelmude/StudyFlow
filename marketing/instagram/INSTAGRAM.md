## 4. The nine-post grid: post after the launch trio

The nine posts alternate navy and light backgrounds, so the profile always
shows a checkerboard. **Post them in number order** (01 first). Instagram puts
the newest post top-left, so 09 ends up top-left and 01 bottom-right, as in
the preview.

You can post all nine on launch day so the profile looks complete from the
start, or post one a day for 9 days. Either way, keep the order.

Each image is 1080×1350 (4:5). The profile grid crops the edges a little,
and the important content sits away from them.

### 04 · The 2-hour rule
`post-04-the-two-hour-rule.png`

```
Study tip: never give one task more than 2 hours in a day. ⏱

Big pieces of work (an IA, an EE chapter) get spread across the week instead of crammed into one panicked Sunday. Your brain keeps working on it between sessions, too.

This is exactly how the StudyFlow planner works. Tell it the hours you're free each day and it builds a 14-day schedule, most urgent work first, capped at 2h per task per day.

Save this for your next heavy week 📌

#studytips #IBDP #studyplanner #timemanagement #IBstudent #studymotivation
```

## 7. After launch: what to post

Aim for **3 posts a week** plus stories most days. Rotate four content types:

| Type | Share | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Study tips** (saveable) | 40% | Pomodoro for IB, "how to pick an EE topic", IA checklists by subject, TOK essay structure |
| **Product** | 25% | 15-second screen recordings: adding a deadline, the planner building a week, ManageBac import |
| **Relatable** | 20% | "IB students during May exams", "POV: three IAs due the same week" |
| **Community** | 15% | Polls, "drop your subjects", question boxes, reposting students' setups (with permission) |

### Reel ideas (Reels reach the most people who don't follow you yet)

1. **"Set up my IB semester in 60 seconds"**: a screen recording of onboarding, sped up, with trending audio.
2. **"POV: you finally see all your deadlines in one place"**: a messy notes app, then the StudyFlow dashboard.
3. **"The 2-hour rule"**: a talking-head or text-on-screen version of post 04.
4. **"Rating IB study methods"**: a tier list, with StudyFlow as the planner tier.
5. **"How I'd plan the EE if I started today"**: walk through the milestones.

### Timing to watch

Post more around the IB year's pressure points: start of term, IA draft
seasons, EE deadlines, mock exams, and the run-up to May exams. That's when
students are actively looking for help.

## 8. Ground rules

- **Only claim what's true.** StudyFlow has no users yet, so no "thousands of students love it" and no made-up reviews. The posts here only state facts about the app.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first weeks. Early engagement tells the algorithm the account is worth showing.
- Instagram allows only one bio link: `https://make-study-flow.netlify.app/`. The marketing page links on to the app.
