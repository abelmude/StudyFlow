The core, covered

Not a generic to-do app. An IB app.

Generic planners don't know what an IA is, or that CAS needs hours and reflections, or that TOK and the EE are graded together. StudyFlow does.

Assignments

Homework, tests and mocks across all six subjects, sorted by what's actually due next — not by when you added it.

Internal Assessments

Break every IA into proposal, data, draft and final. Each stage gets its own deadline so the whole thing never lands at once.

Setup

Three steps. About ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Add your subjects

    Pick your six subjects with HL/SL levels, plus your EE subject. StudyFlow pre-loads the standard IA and TOK milestones for each one.

  2. 2

    Drop in your deadlines

    Type them, paste your school's deadline sheet, or import a calendar file. Anything your teacher moves later, you move once.

  3. 3

    Let the planner run

    Mark when you're free. StudyFlow builds the week, tracks what you finish, and quietly rebuilds it when life gets in the way.

    Questions

    Before you sign up.

    Is StudyFlow only for IB students?

    It's built around the IB Diploma — IAs, the Extended Essay, CAS and TOK are first-class features, not tags you have to invent. A-Level and AP students can use the assignments, calendar and planner happily, but the core requirement tracking is IB-shaped.

    Does it replace ManageBac?

    No — it sits next to it. ManageBac is where your school submits work; StudyFlow is where you plan getting it done. You can import deadlines from a calendar feed so you're not typing everything twice.

    How does the AI planner decide my schedule?

    It uses your deadlines, an estimate of remaining work per task, and the hours you've marked free. Big pieces like the EE are split into sessions across several weeks; short tasks get slotted into gaps. Every block is editable, and nothing is scheduled outside the hours you gave it.

    What happens to my data?

    Your work is yours. StudyFlow doesn't sell student data, and you can export everything — deadlines, CAS log, progress history — as a file you keep, at any time.

    Is it really free?

    The core organizer is free for students: unlimited subjects, deadlines, calendars and CAS tracking. A paid tier covers heavier AI planning and exportable reports, and it stays optional.
